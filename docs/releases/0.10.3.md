Map selection and extreme weather fixes.

- Tapping something on the map selects it first. A card shows what it is with its own actions: focus, target, fly, add a burn or warp there. Orbit points show a countdown with altitude and speed. Overlapping markers give a list to choose from.
- Volcanic ash drifts with the wind, on the map and in the warnings too. From high up and in orbit you can see it as a veil.
- Jets flame out in ash and show FLAMEOUT. Helicopter rotors lose lift and slow down, and show ROTOR LOW.
- Time warp stops ahead of a ground blizzard.
- Lightning bolts no longer loop near the top, hail has a dark rim, and tsunami crest lines stay straight along the coast and at the shelf edge.
- No antialiasing on LOW detail.
- Weather is worked out the same way on every device.
- The documentation is rewritten in plainer words.

Multiplayer uses protocol 38, so everyone in a game needs this build. Terrain generation is still 12 and existing saves still work.
