Builder controls, desktop interface sizing and terrain fixes.

- Builder shortcuts leave typing and dialogs alone. Stack attachment points are easier to pick near side attachment points.
- The craft picker scrolls properly on touchscreens.
- Desktop interface size follows the display, with a manual setting from 100% to 250%.
- Terrain patch edges no longer show dark seams from high up.
- Near-ground orbit paths are easier to select over a world's disk on the map.
- Helicopter rotor animation and sound stay smooth through staging in ash.
- Tsunami sea crests on MEDIUM and HIGH detail no longer form a closed loop near the shore. LOW keeps the original foam.
- The Sparkler can launch from Vehicle Assembly. Solid motors use their own fuel tanks when checking a design.
